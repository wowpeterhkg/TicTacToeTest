# Removal rule

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What happens when the board fills with no line?

## Answer

A removal phase:

1. An RNG picks the total number of pieces to remove, 2–5, uniform.
2. Removal starts with the player who did not place the last piece. An RNG decides how many of the remaining total that player removes; then the other player. Either colour may be removed.
3. When the total is removed, placement resumes with the player after the last remover.

Rejected along the way: last placer removes 2–3 of their choice (X always places the 9th piece, so X always holds the power; loops); alternating 1-at-a-time removal (fixes the X bias, still loops).

Exact split mechanics (zero rolls, remainder handling) are open in [Removal details](06-removal-details.md). Rule is pending verification by the [Rule checker](05-rule-checker.md).
